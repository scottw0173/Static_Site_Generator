# A Bit About Me

[< Back Home](/)

Outside of work and code, I am someone who is happiest when I am close to animals,
moving through the world, or elbow-deep in an engine bay of a car older than me. I have spent time underwater on coral reefs, behind the wheel of old wagons on long drives, and on trails in places
that reminded me why any of it matters. The pets are non-negotiable.
