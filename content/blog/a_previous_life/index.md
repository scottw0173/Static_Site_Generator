# A Previous Life

[< Back Home](/)

For more than a decade I built and maintained aquariums professionally. First as a
technician, then as the owner of Madness Reefing in the Oklahoma City metro area.
At its peak the business maintained 30 aquariums across 22 accounts, and operated
out of a 2,000 sqft coral farm housing over 1,500 gallons of live reef systems.
I'm proud of the work and the relationships built along the way — with clients,
with employees, with the animals, and with the reefs themselves.
